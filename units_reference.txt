AUTOCHEST — UNIT REFERENCE
==========================
Stats note: ATK SPD = attacks per second. Range = attack range in cells (0 = melee).
Upgrades: each unit has a tree of 3 upgrades; players may purchase up to 2 per round.
==========================

CALCIUM CLAN  (Undead skeletons)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

──────────────────────────────────
BONEY
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Calcium Clan
Tags:      Undead, Brawler
Archetype: Berserker
Cost:      2 coins   |   Rarity: Common
Type:      Melee
HP: 7   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 1.0/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A ragged skeleton warrior held together by spite. Not particularly tough,
  but the closer it gets to death the more dangerous it becomes. Best used as
  a cheap frontline threat that punishes enemies for letting it survive.

Passive — Berserker
  Deals 2× damage while below 50% HP.

Upgrade 1 — Mend
  Heals 25% max HP the moment HP drops below 50% (one time per round).

Upgrade 2 — Fury
  +50% attack speed while below 50% HP.

Upgrade 3 — Desperate
  Damage multiplier while low HP becomes 3× instead of 2×.

──────────────────────────────────
MARROW
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Calcium Clan
Tags:      Undead, Marksman
Archetype: Skirmisher
Cost:      2 coins   |   Rarity: Common
Type:      Ranged
HP: 9   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 1.05/s   Move: 1/s   Range: 3 cells

Description:
  A skeletal archer who opens every battle with a powerful lance throw down
  its column before switching to rapid ranged fire. Cheap and consistent —
  the lance alone can cripple a frontline unit before the fight even begins.

Passive — Lance Throw
  At battle start, fires a lance down its column dealing 5 damage to the
  first enemy hit.

Upgrade 1 — Extended Range
  +1 attack range (4 total).

Upgrade 2 — Momentum
  After killing an enemy, gains +50% damage for 2 seconds.

Upgrade 3 — Volley
  Each attack fires two arrows simultaneously, hitting a second enemy in range.

──────────────────────────────────
MEND
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Calcium Clan
Tags:      Undead, Support
Archetype: Healer
Cost:      3 coins   |   Rarity: Common
Type:      Ranged
HP: 8   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 0.8/s   Move: 1/s   Range: 3 cells

Description:
  A skeletal field medic carrying a gnarled staff. It fights at range but
  its real value is its pulse heal — periodically it channels restorative
  energy to whichever ally is hurting most. Keep it alive and your whole
  front line becomes harder to wear down.

Passive — Mending Pulse
  Every 6 combined hits (dealt + received), heals the lowest HP ally for 2 HP.

Upgrade 1 — Mending Touch
  Heal amount increased to 3 HP.

Upgrade 2 — Invigorating Pulse
  The healed ally also gains +15% attack speed for 2 seconds.

Upgrade 3 — Double Dose
  Heal the two lowest HP allies instead of just one.

──────────────────────────────────
AMALGAM
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Calcium Clan
Tags:      Undead, Monster
Archetype: Juggernaut
Cost:      4 coins   |   Rarity: Common
Type:      Melee
HP: 13   DMG: 3   ATK SPD: 0.45/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A horrific mass of fused bones, sinew, and cursed will. Virtually
  impossible to put down in one blow — its stitched-together body snaps
  back from any single lethal strike, leaving it briefly untouchable.
  Slow and brutal; built to soak punishment and keep swinging.

Passive — Unholy Resilience
  Cannot die from a single hit while above 1 HP. Surviving a lethal blow
  reduces HP to 1 and grants 1 second of full invulnerability (7 second
  cooldown).

Upgrade 1 — Bone Armor
  Permanently reduces all incoming damage by 15% (minimum 1).

Upgrade 2 — Corpse Explosion
  When invulnerability triggers, deals 2× base damage to all enemies within
  2 cells.

Upgrade 3 — Relentless
  Invulnerability cooldown reduced from 7 seconds to 6 seconds.

──────────────────────────────────
CLAVICULA
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Calcium Clan
Tags:      Undead, Brawler
Archetype: Sustain Fighter
Cost:      4 coins   |   Rarity: Rare
Type:      Melee
HP: 14   DMG: 2   ATK SPD: 0.9/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A whirling skeleton berserker that thrives in the thick of the fight.
  Absorbs punishment and channels it into explosive spinning attacks that
  damage every adjacent enemy at once. The more it's surrounded, the more
  it heals — making it extremely dangerous to gang up on.

Passive — Whirlwind
  Every 10 combined hits (dealt + received), spins and strikes all adjacent
  enemies for full damage, then heals 50% of total damage dealt.

Upgrade 1 — Bloodwhirl
  Whirlwind heals 75% of damage dealt instead of 50%.

Upgrade 2 — Frenzy
  Whirlwind triggers every 7 hits instead of 10.

Upgrade 3 — War Spoils
  On every kill, permanently gains +1 damage for the rest of the round (stacks).

──────────────────────────────────
HUMERUS
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Calcium Clan
Tags:      Undead, Brawler
Archetype: Commander / Tank
Cost:      5 coins   |   Rarity: Rare
Type:      Melee
HP: 22   DMG: 4   ATK SPD: 0.35/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  The towering bone lord of the Calcium Clan. Slow and devastating, with
  the highest HP pool in the game. Its mere presence on the battlefield
  sharpens the strikes of every ally focused on the same target, turning
  coordinated attacks into a death sentence. A cornerstone unit that anchors
  the whole team.

Passive — Royal Command
  Allies attacking the same target as Humerus gain +20% damage.

Upgrade 1 — Bone Throne
  Heals 15% of max HP after every kill.

Upgrade 2 — Execute
  Deals +100% damage to enemies below 30% HP.

Upgrade 3 — Cleave
  Every 3rd attack also strikes the units flanking the primary target
  (same row, adjacent column).

──────────────────────────────────
MIGRAINE
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Calcium Clan
Tags:      Undead, Marksman
Archetype: Swarmer / Replicator
Cost:      4 coins   |   Rarity: Epic
Type:      Ranged
HP: 9   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 0.65/s   Move: 1/s   Range: 3 cells

Description:
  A spectral skeleton that refuses to stay whole. Whenever it absorbs or
  deals enough punishment, it tears itself in two — spawning a copy that
  carries on the fight. Left unchecked it floods the board with clones,
  overwhelming enemies through sheer volume. Hard to focus down because
  killing one just makes room for the others.

Passive — Spectral Mitosis
  Every 8 combined hits (dealt + received), spawns a copy of itself on a
  nearby free cell, splitting current HP 50/50 with the original. Max 4
  Migraines per player on the board at once.

Upgrade 1 — Twin Spirits
  Copies spawn at 65% of current HP instead of 50%.

Upgrade 2 — Soul Drain
  When any Migraine (original or clone) dies, all surviving copies heal 25%
  of max HP.

Upgrade 3 — Cursed Ground
  Migraine and all clones explode on death, dealing 3 damage to enemies within
  2 cells and leaving a burning cross on the ground for 2 seconds.

──────────────────────────────────
TOMB
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Calcium Clan
Tags:      Undead
Archetype: Utility Structure / Anchor
Cost:      1 coin   |   Rarity: Epic
Type:      Stationary (cannot move or attack)
HP: 15   DMG: 0   ATK SPD: —   Move: 0   Range: —

Description:
  An ancient gravestone that has wandered onto the battlefield. It cannot
  move or fight, but it channels dark energy from every corpse that falls
  nearby. Allies who pass over the dead are healed, turning the graveyard
  into a regeneration zone. Its absurdly low cost makes it a tempting filler
  pick — but its epic rarity means it's not found often.

Passive — Corpse Channel
  Friendly units that step onto a cell where a corpse lies heal 2 HP
  (once per unit per corpse position per round).

Upgrade 1 — Rot
  Friendly units also regen 1 HP per second while standing on a corpse cell.

Upgrade 2 — Martyrdom
  When Tomb dies, all surviving friendly units gain +25% attack speed for 4
  seconds.

Upgrade 3 — Reinforced
  +5 base HP (scales with level).

KNIGHT
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Castle Crew
Tags:      Folk, Support
Archetype: Protector / Tank
Cost:      3 coins   |   Rarity: Common
Type:      Melee
HP: 12   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 1.0/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A heavily armored defender who leads by example. The moment battle starts,
  the Knight issues a war cry that forces nearby enemies to focus on it,
  shielding more fragile allies from harm. Not the biggest threat on the
  board, but essential for buying time for your damage dealers.

Passive — Taunt
  At battle start, forces all enemies within 3 cells to target Knight for
  3 seconds.

Upgrade 1 — Iron Will
  Taunt duration extended to 5 seconds.

Upgrade 2 — Guardian
  Gains +5 max HP (and current HP) for each enemy caught in the taunt radius.

Upgrade 3 — Mend
  Heals 25% max HP the moment HP drops below 50% (one time per round).

MARC
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Castle Crew
Tags:      Folk, Marksman
Archetype: Sniper
Cost:      3 coins   |   Rarity: Common
Type:      Ranged
HP: 10   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 0.75/s   Move: 1/s   Range: 6 cells

Description:
  A cold, patient sharpshooter with the longest attack range in the game.
  While other archers aim at whoever is closest, Marc locks onto the furthest
  target in sight — punishing enemies who hide behind their frontline and
  picking off squishy backrow units before they can act.

Passive — Sniper Focus
  When enemies are in attack range, always targets the furthest one rather
  than the nearest.

Upgrade 1 — Headshot
  +50% damage against enemies above 80% HP.

Upgrade 2 — Piercing Arrow
  Every 3rd attack fires a piercing arrow that passes through all enemies in
  a straight line.

Upgrade 3 — Eagle Eye
  +2 attack range (8 total).

──────────────────────────────────
MAGE
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Castle Crew
Tags:      Folk, Marksman
Archetype: Battle Mage / Burst Caster
Cost:      4 coins   |   Rarity: Common
Type:      Ranged
HP: 9   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 0.55/s   Move: 1/s   Range: 3 cells

Description:
  A robed caster who fights at medium range, channeling energy from every
  exchange into a devastating fireball. Weak individually, but in a sustained
  battle the periodic AoE explosion can wipe out clusters of enemies in an
  instant. Positioning it behind units with hit-counter synergy (or on a busy
  frontline) charges the fireball faster.

Passive — Fireball
  Every 8 combined hits (dealt + received), the next shot is upgraded into a
  fireball that deals 2× damage in a 1-cell AoE on impact.

Upgrade 1 — Burning Ground
  Fireball explosions leave burning ground that deals 1 damage/second for 4
  seconds.

Upgrade 2 — Arcane Surge
  Casting a fireball grants +50% attack speed for 3 seconds.

Upgrade 3 — Big Boom
  Fireball blast radius increased to 2 cells.

BULL
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Castle Crew
Tags:      Folk, Monster
Archetype: Initiator / Disruptor
Cost:      4 coins   |   Rarity: Common
Type:      Melee
HP: 13   DMG: 2   ATK SPD: 0.65/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A raging minotaur that can't wait for the enemy to come to it. The moment
  battle begins it charges forward up to 4 tiles, crashing into the first
  enemy in its path with a stunning blow. Excellent at breaking formation and
  disrupting the enemy backrow before they can set up.

Passive — Charge
  At battle start, charges forward up to 4 tiles. Deals 1.5× damage and stuns
  the first enemy hit for 2 seconds.

Upgrade 1 — Unstoppable
  Charge also knocks the hit enemy one tile backward.

Upgrade 2 — Blazing Trail
  Leaves burning fire on every cell traversed during the charge, plus the
  landing tile and the enemy's tile. Fire lasts 3 seconds, dealing 1 damage/s.

Upgrade 3 — Trampling Hooves
  Charge stun duration increased to 3 seconds.

SAMURAI
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Castle Crew
Tags:      Folk, Brawler
Archetype: Lone Wolf / Assassin
Cost:      3 coins   |   Rarity: Rare
Type:      Melee
HP: 10   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 1.1/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A solitary warrior whose blade sharpens the farther it stands from allies.
  Place it alone on a flank and it strikes twice as hard; surround it with
  teammates and it loses its edge. Designed to be deployed as an isolated
  flanker, it rewards unconventional board positioning and punishes the enemy
  for ignoring a corner of the field.

Passive — Isolation
  Deals 2× damage when no friendly units are within 2 cells.

Upgrade 1 — Bloodthirst
  Heals 30% max HP on kill.

Upgrade 2 — Vengeance
  Gains +1 permanent damage for every ally that dies within 2 cells
  (stacks throughout the round).

Upgrade 3 — Lone Wolf
  Isolation damage multiplier becomes 1.75× (trades some raw isolation bonus;
  pairs well with Vengeance stacking for late-round damage).

──────────────────────────────────
BONK
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Castle Crew
Tags:      Folk, Brawler
Archetype: Heavy Hitter / Counter Pick
Cost:      3 coins   |   Rarity: Rare
Type:      Melee
HP: 11   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 0.7/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A stocky brawler lugging an oversized blunt weapon. Steady and reliable,
  it winds up every few swings for a bone-crushing blow that hits far harder
  than anything else its size. A natural counter to units that rely on hit
  counters — each swing chips away at their mechanics while building toward
  its own Power Strike.

Passive — Power Strike
  Every 3rd hit deals 3× damage.

Upgrade 1 — Brute Force
  Power Strike triggers every 4th hit instead of 3rd but also stuns the
  target for 1.5 seconds.

Upgrade 2 — Cleave
  Power Strike also hits units in adjacent side columns, applying all other
  active upgrades (stun, counter drain) to cleaved targets.

Upgrade 3 — Counter Crush
  Every hit removes 1 hit counter from the target (hard counters Mage,
  Clavicula, Migraine, Sinner, and any other unit using hit-counter mechanics).

NINJA
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Castle Crew
Tags:      Folk, Brawler
Archetype: Assassin / Flanker
Cost:      4 coins   |   Rarity: Rare
Type:      Melee
HP: 11   DMG: 2   ATK SPD: 1.2/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A swift shadow-warrior who thrives in the chaos of a packed battlefield.
  Patient and deliberate, she counts every blow traded until the moment
  strikes — then vanishes across the board in a blur, reappearing next to
  the enemy she least wants to fight. Frail if pinned down, devastating
  when allowed to pick her targets.

Passive — Shadow Dash
  Every 7 combined hits (dealt + received), dashes to the farthest enemy
  unit on the board and locks onto them as the new target for 5 seconds
  (or until they die). Energy resets after each dash.

Upgrade 1 — Phantom Cut
  Enemies along the dash path take damage equal to Ninja's base damage.

Upgrade 2 — Chi Drain
  The dash removes 2 energy (hit counter) from every enemy dashed through.

Upgrade 3 — Smokescreen
  On landing, stuns all enemies within 1.5 cells for 0.8 seconds.

SINNER
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Castle Crew
Tags:      Folk, Brawler
Archetype: Berserker (Delayed)
Cost:      4 coins   |   Rarity: Epic
Type:      Melee
HP: 18   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 0.6/s   Move: 0.8/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A prisoner wrapped in heavy chains who starts the fight sluggish and
  suppressed. Absorb enough hits and the chains shatter — releasing a true
  berserker that moves faster, attacks faster, and cannot be stunned for the
  rest of the round. High HP pool means it usually survives long enough to
  transform; the question is whether it does so before the battle is decided.

Passive — Break Free
  After 15 combined hits (dealt + received), breaks its chains permanently:
  ATK SPD jumps from 0.6 to 1.3/s and Sinner becomes immune to stun.

Upgrade 1 — Early Release
  Break Free triggers at 10 hits instead of 15.

Upgrade 2 — Mending Chains
  Heals 40% max HP upon breaking free.

Upgrade 3 — Shattered Chains
  Upon breaking free, stuns all enemies within 2 cells for 0.5 seconds.

CATAPULT
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Castle Crew
Tags:      Folk
Archetype: Siege / Area Denier
Cost:      2 coins   |   Rarity: Epic
Type:      Stationary (cannot move or attack normally)
HP: 12   DMG: 3   ATK SPD: —   Move: 0   Range: —

Description:
  A siege engine bolted to the ground. It never moves and never swings, but
  it doesn't need to — the moment battle starts it launches a single massive
  volley that carpets a cross-shaped area deep in enemy territory with
  explosive damage and burning ground. After that it just sits as a durable
  HP block, daring the enemy to come finish it off while its fire does the work.

Passive — Bombardment
  At battle start, fires a projectile 4 rows forward. On impact, deals 3
  damage in a cross pattern (5 cells: center + 4 cardinal neighbors) and
  leaves burning ground on all 5 cells for 5 seconds (1 damage/second).

Upgrade 1 — Ember Drain
  Burning ground also removes 1 hit counter per damage tick from targets with
  hit-counter mechanics.

Upgrade 2 — Scorched
  Enemies standing on burning ground cannot be healed.

Upgrade 3 — Heavy Payload
  Initial explosion damage increased from 3 to 5.

BURROW
──────────────────────────────────
Clan:      Goblin Gang
Tags:      Monster, Brawler
Archetype: Flanker / Ambusher
Cost:      3 coins   |   Rarity: Common
Type:      Melee
HP: 8   DMG: 1   ATK SPD: 0.75/s   Move: 1/s   Range: Melee

Description:
  A wiry goblin tunneler who doesn't believe in fighting fair. The instant
  battle begins it dives underground, vanishing completely, and resurfaces
  2 seconds later at the mirrored position on the far side of the field —
  right in the middle of the enemy backrow. Fragile, but the repositioning
  alone can completely ruin an opponent's formation and draw fire away from
  the rest of your team.

Passive — Subterranean Dash
  At battle start, burrows underground (invisible and invulnerable) for 2
  seconds, then re-emerges at the mirrored row on the far side of the field
  (same column, nearest free cell).

Upgrade 1 — Counter Strike
  Each melee hit removes 1 hit counter from the target.

Upgrade 2 — Ground Burst
  Emerging from the ground deals 2 damage to all enemies within 1.5 cells and
  stuns them for 0.8 seconds.

Upgrade 3 — Adrenaline Surge
  Gains +30% attack speed for 3 seconds after emerging from burrow.
